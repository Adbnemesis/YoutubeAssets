Caption:
How do you calculate trapped rainwater in an irregular elevation map without testing every single block? 🌧️

Most people allocate two auxiliary arrays for Left-Max and Right-Max, but that consumes O(N) extra RAM! 😱

Here is why Two Pointers solve it in constant O(1) space:
• Water height is strictly limited by the shorter wall
• Because the taller wall guarantees containment, we only advance the pointer with the smaller maximum!
• Left and Right march inward, trapping water in every valley in a single linear pass ⚡
