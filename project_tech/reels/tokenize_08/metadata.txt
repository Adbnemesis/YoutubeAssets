Why does ChatGPT fail at counting the 'r's in "strawberry"? 🔍🤖
It's not broken — it's the Byte-Pair Tokenizer! Large Language Models never see individual letters. Words are sliced into subword chunks and mapped directly to integers from a 100,000-entry dictionary. "strawberry" becomes [496] + [675], locking the letters away in high-dimensional vector space.
