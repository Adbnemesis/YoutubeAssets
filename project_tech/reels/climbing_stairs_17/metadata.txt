Why does solving Climbing Stairs with pure recursion melt your CPU? 🪜💥

If you test every branch recursively, you recalculate the exact same sub-problems billions of times in O(2^N) exponential disaster! 😱

Here is how Dynamic Programming solves it in O(1) space:
• To reach Step N, you either took 1 step from N-1, or 2 steps from N-2
• That means ways(N) = ways(N-1) + ways(N-2) — it's literally Fibonacci! ⚡
• You only need to track the last two numbers: sliding two variables up the staircase in a single linear pass! 🚀
