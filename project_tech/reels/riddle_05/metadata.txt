Caption:
Can you solve this 50-year-old deadly riddle? 🤔🍜

Five masters sit at a circular table. To eat, each master needs two chopsticks. Every master grabs the chopstick to their left simultaneously... and now all five wait forever for the right one. Nobody drops theirs, and all five starve.

What invisible curse just killed them? 💀

It's a DEADLOCK. In computer science, this is the exact nightmare that freezes multi-threaded systems: Thread A holds Lock 1 waiting for Lock 2, while Thread B holds Lock 2 waiting for Lock 1.

The 4 Coffman Conditions:
1️⃣ Mutual Exclusion
2️⃣ Hold & Wait
3️⃣ No Preemption
4️⃣ Circular Wait

Break ANY one condition, and the deadlock is broken! 💡
