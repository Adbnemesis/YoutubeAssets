# NEMI EXPLAINS — STORY ARCHETYPES & NARRATIVE SCHEMAS

The Nemi Explains Content Engine categorizes every technical topic into one of **8 core story archetypes**.
Each archetype specifies the exact emotional progression, camera choreography, mascot behavior, and beat schema.

---

## 1. ARCHETYPE 1 — HIDDEN JOURNEY
* **Concept:** An invisible entity (request, packet, transaction) travels through a physical network.
* **Core Emotion:** Curiosity $\to$ Momentum $\to$ Wonder $\to$ Payoff.
* **Beat Structure:**
  1. `Input & Launch` (Immediate action in client UI)
  2. `Address Resolution` (Directory lookup / DNS / Routing table)
  3. `Network Traversal` (Camera tracks entity across 3D nodes)
  4. `Destination Processing` (Datacenter / Server handling)
  5. `Return Assembly` (Data packet returns & paints UI)
  6. `Outro Payoff` (High-density 3-point takeaway)
* **Example Topics:** *What happens when you type google.com?*, *How a credit card payment settles in 2 seconds*, *How an email travels across the Atlantic*.

---

## 2. ARCHETYPE 2 — MYSTERY (V12 Standard)
* **Concept:** A counter-intuitive or broken result occurs. We investigate the hidden machinery to reveal the truth.
* **Core Emotion:** Confusion $\to$ Curiosity $\to$ Investigation $\to$ The "Aha!" Discovery.
* **Beat Structure:**
  1. `The Impossible Result` (Code/machine produces an unexpected output)
  2. `Mascot Reaction` (Nemi: *"What?!"* — validates viewer's confusion)
  3. `The Deep Dive` (Camera pushes into internal bit/hardware world)
  4. `The Root Cause Mechanism` (Visual demonstration of the hidden constraint)
  5. `The "Aha!" Reveal` (Machine didn't fail—it followed a secret rule)
  6. `Takeaway Payoff` (Concise rule & celebratory mascot)
* **Example Topics:** *Why 0.1 + 0.2 is not 0.3*, *Why JavaScript has NaN === NaN as false*, *Why arrays start at index 0*.

---

## 3. ARCHETYPE 3 — CHALLENGE / RIDDLE
* **Concept:** A short puzzle is presented to the viewer. Nemi makes a wrong guess, followed by a surprise reversal.
* **Core Emotion:** Competitive Curiosity $\to$ Wrong Guess $\to$ Surprise $\to$ Technical Clarity.
* **Beat Structure:**
  1. `The Puzzle Code` (Short snippet or system diagram)
  2. `Viewer Inspection Pause` (0.6s–0.8s mental participation window)
  3. `Mascot Guess` (Nemi points confidently at wrong choice)
  4. `The Reversal Freeze` (Narrator reveals the hidden edge case)
  5. `Execution Simulation` (Visual step-by-step proof)
  6. `Winner Takeaway`
* **Example Topics:** *Spot the Bug*, *Output Predictor*, *Which object gets deleted?*.

---

## 4. ARCHETYPE 4 — TRANSFORMATION (V10 Standard)
* **Concept:** A chaotic or bloated state is reorganized into an efficient, clean state.
* **Core Emotion:** Overwhelm $\to$ Mechanism Discovery $\to$ Cleanup Climax $\to$ Relief.
* **Beat Structure:**
  1. `The Mess` (Cascading objects / fragmentation / memory spike)
  2. `The Dilemma` (Who cleans this up?)
  3. `Reachability / Rule` (Green alive vs Red garbage)
  4. `Cleanup Climax` (Rhythmic deletion pop sequence)
  5. `Defragmentation Snap` (Visual compression into clean solid block)
  6. `Payoff`
* **Example Topics:** *Garbage Collection*, *Quicksort partitioning*, *Database B-tree rebalancing*, *Gzip compression*.

---

## 5. ARCHETYPE 5 — BEHIND THE SCENES
* **Concept:** An everyday feature everyone takes for granted is revealed to have colossal infrastructure behind it.
* **Core Emotion:** Familiarity $\to$ Surprising Scale $\to$ Admiration.
* **Beat Structure:** Simple Click $\to$ Hidden Global Infrastructure $\to$ Microsecond Pipeline $\to$ Magic Result.
* **Example Topics:** *How Google autocomplete predicts your thought in 12ms*, *How Shazam recognizes a song in 3 seconds*, *How CAPTCHAs know you are human from a mouse wiggle*.

---

## 6. ARCHETYPE 6 — WRONG ASSUMPTION
* **Concept:** Everyone believes a technical fact works one way, but under the hood, it works completely opposite.
* **Core Emotion:** Certainty $\to$ Doubt $\to$ Proof $\to$ Mind Blown.
* **Example Topics:** *Why RAM is NOT always faster than SSD*, *Why writing `async/await` doesn't make code run in parallel*, *Why deleting a file doesn't actually erase data*.

---

## 7. ARCHETYPE 7 — THE ALGORITHM DUEL (Comparison)
* **Concept:** Two algorithms or architectures race head-to-head on the same dataset.
* **Core Emotion:** Competition $\to$ Visual Tension $\to$ Victorious Payoff.
* **Example Topics:** *Bubble Sort vs Quick Sort*, *HTTP/1.1 vs HTTP/3 Multiplexing*, *SQL vs NoSQL scaling*.

---

## 8. ARCHETYPE 8 — ESCALATION
* **Concept:** A simple feature works for 1 user, breaks at 1,000, explodes at 1,000,000, requiring architectural evolution.
* **Core Emotion:** Simplicity $\to$ Pressure $\to$ Crisis $\to$ Elegant Engineering Solution.
* **Example Topics:** *How to scale from 1 to 10 million users*, *Why single-thread JavaScript handles 100k requests*, *Why microservices exist*.
