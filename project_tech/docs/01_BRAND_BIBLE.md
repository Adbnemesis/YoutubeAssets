# NEMI EXPLAINS — BRAND BIBLE & IDENTITY

## 1. Brand Identity
* **Brand Name:** `Nemi Explains`
* **Social Handle:** `@nemi.explains`
* **Core Tagline:** *"Making the technology behind our world impossible not to understand."*
* **Channel Archetype:** *The Curious Engineer & The Master Animator.*

---

## 2. Core Brand Values
1. **Intellectual Honesty:** Never dumb down real CS concepts into false analogies. Show the real memory, pointers, nodes, and registers.
2. **Zero Waste (No Fluff):** No "Hey guys welcome back", no 3-second title animations, no generic intro graphics. Frame 0 is the hook.
3. **Warm Premium Aesthetic:** Reject cliché generic dark mode and sterile corporate templates. Use bespoke designer cream surfaces, charcoal cards, and vibrant semantic accents.
4. **Relatable Mascot:** Nemi represents the viewer — curious, makes honest assumptions, gets surprised, and learns alongside the audience.

---

## 3. Tone of Voice
* **Narrator:** Calm, authoritative, concise, slightly dramatic when unveiling mechanisms, clear and conversational.
* **Nemi:** Energetic, curious, punchy ("Wait... Oh", "Because I'm not doing it", "Bye. 👋", "Much better!").
* **Narrative Relationship:** The Narrator presents the technical universe; Nemi lives inside it as the viewer's avatar.

---

## 4. Strict Anti-Topics & Prohibitions
* ❌ No generic influencer lifestyle / "Day in the life of a FAANG engineer"
* ❌ No clickbait get-rich-quick "Make $10k with AI"
* ❌ No elementary beginner syntax tutorials ("What is a string?")
* ❌ No unverified tech drama or celebrity gossip
* ❌ No generic meme reposting
