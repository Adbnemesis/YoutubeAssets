# Garbage Collection — Reel Case Studies (V01 to V07)

## Version V01
* **Objective:** Initial Remotion proof-of-concept for Garbage Collection.
* **Format:** Multiple choice quiz format.
* **Duration:** 25.0s (750 frames).
* **Audio:** Silent / Sound effects only.
* **Outcome:** Failed retention standard. Format was too boring and academic.

---

## Version V02
* **Objective:** Emulate visual benchmark of `ThingsExplained.mp4` with custom branding.
* **Format:** Laser scanner sweep and mark-and-sweep tree.
* **Duration:** 29.0s (870 frames).
* **Audio:** Synthwave BGM only.
* **Outcome:** High visual score (8.5/10), but social retention remained low due to lack of narration.

---

## Version V03
* **Objective:** Add narrative voiceover using `tech_voice_profile.json`.
* **Format:** Scripted narration + visual timeline.
* **Duration:** 33.5s (1006 frames).
* **Outcome:** Audio was not properly wired into the final Remotion MP4 export.

---

## Version V04
* **Objective:** Hard-link Chatterbox voiceover into Remotion rendering.
* **Format:** Full Chatterbox speech track.
* **Duration:** 34.4s (1032 frames).
* **Audio:** Spoken voice verified in output MP4.
* **Outcome:** Voice was too quiet relative to music, pacing was sluggish, and awkward silence gaps existed.

---

## Version V05
* **Objective:** Audio mastering with silence trimming, -16 LUFS voice norm, and sidechain ducking.
* **Format:** Data-driven cues in `nemi_v5_cues.json`.
* **Duration:** 25.8s (775 frames).
* **Audio:** Clear broadcast mix (-14.8 LUFS master).
* **Outcome:** Audio problems solved, but storytelling was still a linear presentation.

---

## Version V06
* **Objective:** Creative re-architecture around story beats (Hook → Question → Challenge → Freeze → Tree → Cleanup → Compaction).
* **Format:** 10 visual beats, pointing pose, interactive memory challenge.
* **Duration:** 25.2s (756 frames).
* **Outcome:** High retention (94/100), strong character engagement. Minor layout spacing adjustments identified.

---

## Version V07 (Approved Production Master)
* **Objective:** Cohesive 5-block natural phrasing narration, pixel-perfect layout geometry, and complete living documentation.
* **Format:** Full 10-beat micro-story with high visual density.
* **Duration:** 22.08s (662 frames @ 30fps).
* **Audio:** Master -14.0 LUFS, Voice -16.0 LUFS, True Peak -1.5 dBTP.
* **Outcome:** Master release ready for publishing across Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikTok.
