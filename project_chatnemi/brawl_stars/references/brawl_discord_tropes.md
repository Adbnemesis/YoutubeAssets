# 🏆 Brawl Stars Discord Skit Tropes & Comedy Bible

This reference guide documents the core comedic dynamics, running gags, match situations, and sound design patterns for Brawl Stars Discord skits.

---

## 1. Classic Character Dynamics & Rivalries

### 1. Edgar vs Mortis: The Toxic Assassin Dispute
- **The Trope:** Both are "bad random" icons. When they lose a match together, neither takes accountability.
- **Edgar's Stance:** Claims he "carried" because he had 1 kill, spams 👎 pin, insists his jump was high IQ.
- **Mortis's Stance:** Claims his missed trickshot was a "tactical fake", blames 500ms lag, insists Edgar stole his power cubes.
- **Key Reaction SFX:** `vine_boom.mp3`, `edgar_punch.mp3`, `pin_surrender.mp3`.

### 2. Shelly vs Everyone: The Bush Menace
- **The Trope:** Shelly is quiet in the group chat until someone mentions they walked past the center bush in Cavern Churn or Feast or Famine.
- **The Reveal:** Shelly drops an in-game screenshot showing she has had Super + Hypercharge ready for 4 minutes without moving a millimeter.
- **Key Reaction SFX:** `record_scratch.mp3`, `shelly_super.mp3`, `bruh.mp3`.

### 3. Frank: The Interrupted Intellectual
- **The Trope:** Frank tries to write a multi-paragraph Discord post analyzing team compositions, draft pick strategy, and map control.
- **The Gag:** Before he can finish typing or make his point, another brawler (Shelly, Gale, or Surge) interrupts him with a 1-word text and a loud SFX, referencing how Frank gets his Super cancelled mid-swing in every match.
- **Key Reaction SFX:** `error.mp3`, `fahhh.mp3`, `whoosh.mp3`.

### 4. Hank: "I WAS PRAWN READY!"
- **The Trope:** Hank talks entirely in military drill-sergeant cadence and refuses to acknowledge any mistakes.
- **The Gag:** Hank blames the loss on the teammates not being "prawn ready" or not maintaining military bubble formation.
- **Key Reaction SFX:** `anime-wow.mp3`, `brawl_match_lose.mp3`.

### 5. Starr Park Moderator: The Dark Reality
- **The Trope:** The server has an automated bot representing Starr Park corporate.
- **The Gag:** Whenever brawlers argue about real-world logic, game glitches, or losing trophies, the Starr Park bot drops creepy corporate reassurance, immediately subtracts -150 Elo, or server-mutes the complainer.
- **Key Reaction SFX:** `supercell_jingle.mp3`, `discord_leave.mp3`, `vine_boom.mp3`.

## 2. Iconic Match Scenarios to Script

1. **The Brawl Ball Own-Goal Defense:**
   - Opening: Mortis asks why everyone left the team lobby.
   - Escalation: Edgar posts a replay clip showing Mortis dashing into the goal with 2 seconds left on the clock.
   - Climax: Mortis calls Edgar to explain the geometry of the bounce.
   - Punchline: Shelly points out the enemy team wasn't even spawned.

2. **The Solo Showdown Teaming Betrayal:**
   - Opening: Crow asks why Edgar spun in circles with him at 10 trophies, then jumped on his head 3 seconds later.
   - Escalation: Edgar claims "spinning means fight me bro".
   - Climax: Leon posts a screenshot showing he watched the entire betrayal while invisible.
   - Punchline: Shelly blows up both of them from the nearby bush.

3. **Ranked Draft Disasters:**
   - Opening: Team asks why someone picked Level 9 Edgar on Timed Detonation / Open Shooting Star.
   - Escalation: Edgar says "I have the hypercharge skin bro it gives +50 damage".
   - Climax: Frank calculates the statistical impossibility of winning.
   - Punchline: Match starts and ends in 12 seconds with -200 Elo.
