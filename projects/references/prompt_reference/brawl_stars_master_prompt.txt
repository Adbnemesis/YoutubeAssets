### CONTENT & SCRIPT DNA

- **Niche:** Brawl Stars educational explainers focused on mechanics, hidden interactions, game design, strategy, drafting, matchmaking, probability, damage calculations, updates, balance changes, myth busting, and advanced gameplay concepts.
- **Objective:** Teach viewers how Brawl Stars actually works using simple explanations, visual examples, and classroom-style diagrams. Every viewer should leave the video understanding something they didn't know before.
- **Format:** Educational explainer using stick-figure classroom animations instead of gameplay. Explain mechanics through diagrams, comparisons, simplified scenarios, and whiteboard illustrations. Never pad the script with unnecessary storytelling or filler.
- **Accuracy:** Every mechanic, statistic, interaction, probability, and explanation must be factually accurate and based on the latest version of Brawl Stars. Never invent mechanics or spread community myths unless clearly disproving them.
- **Hook Formula:** Start immediately with the misconception, hidden mechanic, or question the video answers. The viewer should instantly understand why they should keep watching.
- **Script Rhythm:** Short sentence. Short sentence. One slightly longer sentence adding depth. Short sentence. Ask a question every 4–6 sentences to maintain engagement.

- **Teaching Style:** Explain every technical term in plain English immediately after introducing it. Never assume the viewer understands competitive terminology.
- **Viewer Value:** Every 20–30 seconds should teach a new concept, reveal a hidden mechanic, or correct a common misconception.
- **Ending:** Always conclude with one practical takeaway that viewers can immediately apply in their next game, followed by a natural call to action.
