## Video Description
What did human ancestors actually sound like before language was born? You might picture primitive grunts or deep, roaring animal noises—but science reveals a far stranger reality. 

In this video, we dive deep into the evolutionary journey of the human voice. From U-shaped hyoid bones to ancient organic "subwoofers" that early hominids used to warn of danger, you’ll discover how humans traded raw power for precision to develop speech. We also look at the groundbreaking computer reconstructions of Neanderthal voices—and why they sounded more like a high-pitched squeak than a movie monster!
