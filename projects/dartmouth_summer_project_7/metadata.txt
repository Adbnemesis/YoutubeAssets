## Video Description
Right now, you live in a world shaped by machines. They write code, diagnose diseases, and drive cars. It feels like an unstoppable force that appeared out of nowhere—but the real answer is far stranger. Why did this world-altering technology start as a casual summer project?

In this video, you will discover the hidden history of the 1956 Dartmouth Summer Research Project, where a small group of ambitious scientists set out to solve the human mind in just two months. Meet the legendary figures like John McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, and Claude Shannon, and learn how their dangerous overconfidence birthed the term "Artificial Intelligence." Explore the triumphs of the Logic Theorist and the crushing crash of the first AI Winter, reflecting on whether we are still repeating their 70-year-old mistakes today.
