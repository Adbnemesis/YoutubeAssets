You press the purple button, hear that epic lightning sound effect, and immediately charge straight into the enemy team. Two seconds later, you are looking at a respawn screen. Why does this happen? The most common mistake players make with Hypercharge is assuming it makes them invincible. In reality, activating Hypercharge is often the easiest way to throw a game.

Let us look at the math. A level 11 Edgar has 6600 health. When he activates his Hypercharge, he receives a 15% shield. This increases his effective health to 7764. That is less than one extra shot from a Piper. If you run in a straight line, you are not an unstoppable force. You are just a purple target.

This leads to the second major mistake: tunnel vision. The moment players activate Hypercharge, they stop playing tactically. They stop using walls, they stop dodging, and they focus entirely on trying to get value from their upgraded Super. But why waste the most powerful part of the buff? When you activate Hypercharge, you receive a massive speed boost, usually between 20% and 26%. Instead of immediately using your Super, you should use this speed to position yourself, bait out enemy gadgets, and force them to waste their ammo. Once they are defenseless, that is when you strike.

There is also a hidden mechanic that even seasoned players miss. It is called the Hypercharge charge lock. Normally, your Hypercharge charges at 40% of your regular Super charge rate. However, while your Hypercharge is active, any damage you deal does not contribute to your next Hypercharge. The meter is completely locked. If you dump all your ammo and charge another Super while the purple lightning is active, you get zero progress toward your next Hypercharge. To maximize efficiency, you should try to charge your regular Super before activating Hypercharge. This allows you to use your upgraded Super immediately, and then use your normal attacks after the Hypercharge ends to immediately start building the next one.

Finally, you must learn how to play against Hypercharge. When you see an enemy turn purple, your immediate reaction should be to retreat. Hypercharge only lasts for 5 seconds. If you can use knockbacks, stuns, or simply run behind walls, you can easily waste their entire activation. A wasted Hypercharge is a massive 5000-coin deficit for the enemy team.

Next time you play, treat Hypercharge as a positioning tool first and a damage tool second. Use the speed to take space, wait for the enemy to panic, and only use your Super when you are guaranteed a value play.
