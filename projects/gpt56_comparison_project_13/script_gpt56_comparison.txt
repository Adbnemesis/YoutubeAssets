Imagine you run a massive jungle delivery business. Some days you need to translate complex ancient maps. Other days you just need to sort mail, or organize banana baskets. For all of this, OpenAI gave us three different monkey helpers in their GPT 5.6 family: Sol, Terra, and Luna. They all share the exact same memory size, but their brainpower and banana cost are completely different.

Let’s start with Luna, the speed racer. Luna is like a tiny chimpanzee on a skateboard. She is lightning-fast and works for almost pennies. If you need her to quickly categorize incoming emails or detect simple spam, Luna is your go-to. But don't ask her to solve a complex coding puzzle. She'll get confused and drop the ball.

Next is Terra, the balanced worker. Think of Terra as your reliable, everyday assistant monkey. Terra is smart enough to handle regular agent workflows, write standard emails, and draft simple blog posts. She is the middle ground, giving you solid reasoning without costing too many bananas.

Finally, we have Sol, the grand chieftain of the jungle. Sol is the heavy-lifting gorilla who sits on a throne of books. Sol is expensive and takes a bit longer to think, but has massive reasoning power. If you need to audit security code, design a database, or solve complex logic riddles, Sol is the only one who can do it.

Why not just use Sol for everything? Because you'll run out of bananas in minutes! And why not use Luna for everything? Because she isn't smart enough for complex tasks. Choosing the right tier is all about matching the job to the right brainpower.
